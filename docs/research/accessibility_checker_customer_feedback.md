Introduction to Customer Feedback
=====================================

The Accessibility Checker is an AI-powered tool designed to help small business owners and solo entrepreneurs ensure their websites are compliant with accessibility regulations. To improve the product and increase customer satisfaction, we conducted customer feedback sessions with a diverse group of users.

Methodology
------------

We recruited 20 participants for the customer feedback sessions, including small business owners, solo entrepreneurs, and web developers. The sessions were conducted remotely using video conferencing software and lasted approximately 60 minutes each. During the sessions, we asked participants to use the Accessibility Checker tool and provide feedback on their experience.

Feedback Categories
--------------------

We categorized the feedback into several areas, including:

* **Ease of use**: How easy was it for participants to use the tool and understand the results?
* **Accuracy**: How accurate were the tool's results in identifying accessibility issues?
* **Recommendations**: Were the tool's recommendations for improvement helpful and actionable?
* **User interface**: What did participants like or dislike about the tool's user interface?
* **Features**: Were there any features missing from the tool that participants would like to see?

Key Findings
-------------

### Ease of Use

* 80% of participants found the tool easy to use and navigate.
* 20% of participants experienced some difficulty understanding the results, particularly those with limited technical expertise.

### Accuracy

* 90% of participants reported that the tool accurately identified accessibility issues on their website.
* 10% of participants reported some false positives or false negatives.

### Recommendations

* 85% of participants found the tool's recommendations for improvement helpful and actionable.
* 15% of participants reported that the recommendations were too vague or did not provide enough guidance.

### User Interface

* 75% of participants liked the tool's user interface, citing its simplicity and ease of use.
* 25% of participants suggested improvements to the user interface, including adding more visual cues and making the results more concise.

### Features

* 60% of participants suggested adding more features to the tool, including the ability to scan multiple pages at once and provide more detailed reporting.
* 40% of participants reported that the tool met their needs and did not require additional features.

Actionable Insights
--------------------

Based on the customer feedback sessions, we identified several areas for improvement:

* **Simplify the results**: Provide more concise and easy-to-understand results, particularly for users with limited technical expertise.
* **Improve the user interface**: Add more visual cues and make the results more concise and easy to navigate.
* **Add more features**: Consider adding features such as the ability to scan multiple pages at once and provide more detailed reporting.
* **Provide more guidance**: Offer more detailed guidance and recommendations for improvement, particularly for users who are new to accessibility.

Conclusion
----------

The customer feedback sessions provided valuable insights into the strengths and weaknesses of the Accessibility Checker tool. By addressing the areas for improvement identified in this research, we can increase customer satisfaction and provide a more effective and user-friendly tool for small business owners and solo entrepreneurs. 

Future Development
------------------

To address the feedback and improve the product, we will prioritize the following development tasks:

* Refactor the results page to provide more concise and easy-to-understand information.
* Update the user interface to include more visual cues and improve navigation.
* Develop new features, such as the ability to scan multiple pages at once and provide more detailed reporting.
* Create additional guidance and resources, including tutorials and documentation, to help users improve their website's accessibility.

By implementing these changes, we can improve the overall user experience and provide a more effective tool for ensuring website accessibility compliance.
