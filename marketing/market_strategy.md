# Market Strategy

## Executive Summary
Our goal is to develop a comprehensive marketing strategy for the Accessibility Checker, an AI-powered tool that scans small business websites for accessibility compliance issues and provides recommendations for improvement. We aim to reach 100 users and achieve $1000 Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) within the next 6 months.

## Situation Analysis
The Accessibility Checker is a unique product that addresses a critical need for small businesses to ensure their websites are compliant with accessibility regulations. The market is growing, and the demand for accessibility solutions is increasing. Our target audience is small business owners and solo entrepreneurs who are looking for an easy and affordable way to ensure their websites are compliant.

## Target Market
Our target market is small business owners and solo entrepreneurs who:

* Own a website
* Are concerned about accessibility compliance
* Are looking for an easy and affordable solution
* Are willing to pay for a tool that can help them achieve compliance

## Marketing Objectives
Our marketing objectives are to:

* Reach 100 users within the next 6 months
* Achieve $1000 MRR within the next 6 months
* Position the Accessibility Checker as a leading solution for small businesses
* Build a community of users who can provide feedback and support

## Marketing Strategies
To achieve our marketing objectives, we will implement the following strategies:

### Content Marketing
We will create high-quality, informative content that addresses the needs and concerns of our target audience. This will include:

* Blog posts on accessibility and compliance
* Guides and tutorials on how to use the Accessibility Checker
* Case studies and success stories of businesses that have used the Accessibility Checker
* Webinars and online workshops on accessibility and compliance

### Social Media Marketing
We will use social media platforms to reach our target audience and build a community of users. This will include:

* Creating a presence on Twitter, LinkedIn, and Facebook
* Sharing our content and engaging with our audience
* Running social media ads to reach a wider audience

### Email Marketing
We will build an email list and send regular newsletters to our subscribers. This will include:

* News and updates on the Accessibility Checker
* Tips and resources on accessibility and compliance
* Promotions and discounts for our subscribers

### Paid Advertising
We will run paid ads on Google and social media platforms to reach our target audience. This will include:

* Google Ads targeting small business owners and solo entrepreneurs
* Social media ads targeting our target audience

### Partnerships and Collaborations
We will partner with organizations and businesses that serve our target audience. This will include:

* Partnering with web design and development companies
* Collaborating with accessibility experts and organizations
* Integrating with other tools and services that serve our target audience

## Tactics
To implement our marketing strategies, we will use the following tactics:

* Create a landing page for the Accessibility Checker
* Develop a lead magnet to capture email addresses
* Create a sales funnel to convert leads into users
* Use A/B testing and analytics to optimize our marketing efforts
* Monitor and respond to customer feedback and support requests

## Budget and Resource Allocation
We will allocate our budget and resources as follows:

* Content creation: 30%
* Social media marketing: 20%
* Email marketing: 15%
* Paid advertising: 20%
* Partnerships and collaborations: 15%

## Performance Metrics and Monitoring
We will monitor and track the following performance metrics:

* Website traffic and engagement
* Email open and click-through rates
* Conversion rates and user acquisition
* Customer satisfaction and retention
* Revenue and MRR growth

## Conclusion
Our comprehensive marketing strategy is designed to reach 100 users and achieve $1000 MRR within the next 6 months. By creating high-quality content, leveraging social media and email marketing, running paid ads, and partnering with organizations and businesses, we can position the Accessibility Checker as a leading solution for small businesses and build a community of users who can provide feedback and support.
